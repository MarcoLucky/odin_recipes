Adobong Sitaw Recipe

Description

Adobong Sitaw is a traditional Filipino dish made with green beans cooked in a savory adobo-style sauce.

Ingredients

  • 1 lb green beans, trimmed
  • 2 tbsp cooking oil
  • 1 small onion, chopped
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 tbsp ginger, minced
  • 1 cup tomato sauce
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1/2 tsp black pepper
  • 2 tbsp vinegar
  • 1 tsp sugar
