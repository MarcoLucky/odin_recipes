Sinigang na Bangus Recipe

Description

Sinigang na Bangus is a traditional Filipino dish made with bangus (milkfish) cooked in a sour and savory soup.

Ingredients

  • 1 lb bangus, cleaned and cut into pieces
  • 2 tbsp cooking oil
  • 1 small onion, chopped
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 tbsp ginger, minced
  • 1 cup tomato sauce
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1/2 tsp black pepper
  • 1 tbsp vinegar
  • 1 tsp sugar
