Paksiw Recipe

Description

Paksiw is a traditional Filipino dish made with fish cooked in a savory sauce.

Ingredients

  • 1 lb fish (such as bangus or tilapia)
  • 2 tbsp cooking oil
  • 1 small onion, chopped
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 tbsp ginger, minced
  • 1 cup tomato sauce
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1/2 tsp black pepper
